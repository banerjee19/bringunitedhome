Privacy policy

6 November 2026

This policy describes what Bring United Home collects on bringunitedhome.org, why, and what we do with it. The site is a fan register of pledges. It is not affiliated with Manchester United Football Club. No money is collected here.

What we collect

If you register a pledge, we store your first name, last name, age, email address, country, phone country code, phone number, the amount you would commit, whether you would pay in one payment or in installments, whether you want the pledge shown publicly, your display name if you give one, the time of the pledge, a pledge ID, a verification code, and the status of the pledge.

The form also includes a check, run by Cloudflare Turnstile, that the pledge was submitted by a person. We store the result of that check only long enough to accept or reject the pledge.

How we use it

We use these details to keep the pledge register, to confirm that a verification email matches the pledge, and to contact you if a time comes when the pledged money actually has to be raised. We do not sell the details, rent them, or use them for advertising. We do not send a newsletter.

What other people can see

The public page shows the total amount pledged, the number of supporters, the number of countries, and a map of those countries. Your display name, country, and amount appear on the page only if you tick the public box. Your email, phone number, age, legal name, pledge ID, and verification code are not published.

Who else handles the details

The pledge register is stored in a private Google Sheet. Verification messages you send to pledge@bringunitedhome.org are received by our Zoho mailbox and forwarded to the Gmail account that updates the register. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page loads typefaces from Google Fonts, a map from a public map file, and the person-check from Cloudflare. Those services receive the technical data needed to load the page. They do not receive your pledge unless you submit the form or send the verification email.

How long we keep it

We keep a pledge for as long as the register is running. You can ask us to delete your row, or to correct it, by emailing hello@bringunitedhome.org from the address you used on the form.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to hello@bringunitedhome.org.
