A pledge to own the club. Not a chant, and not a share already bought.

Bring United Home

Manchester United supporters can register what they would pay toward fan ownership. No money is collected. A pledge does not buy a share, a vote, or a promise of profit.

Register your pledge

One person, one future vote. The price of that vote is $5,000, which can be paid in installments once a proper structure exists. Paying more would buy a larger non-voting certificate, up to $50,000. Nothing is due now.
