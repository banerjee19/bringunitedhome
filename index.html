The pledge

Manchester United is a public company with two classes of shares. Buying what trades on the exchange does not buy control. A register of supporters does not buy it either. It shows, in names and amounts, that fans would fund a serious bid if the owners were willing to sell.

The money stays in your account. The number on this page is what people have said they would commit, not what has been paid. If a purchase ever becomes possible, it will need its own legal structure, a published set of rules, and a fresh decision from each person.

Control, if it is ever won, has to stay with members: one person, one vote, held by a body that cannot sell the club for the benefit of its members. A larger pledge does not buy a second vote.

Three promises

  1. No money yetNothing is collected until there is a legal structure and published rules.
  2. No guaranteeA pledge is not a takeover, a share, or a return.
  3. One voteAbove the threshold, every member has the same vote.
