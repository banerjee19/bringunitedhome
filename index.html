The pledge

Manchester United is currently a public company with two classes of shares. Class B shares owned by the Glazers and INEOS gives them 10 times the vote compared to an ordinary shareholder. They would not voluntarily give that up. The only way to change that is to buy them off. This pledge is a show of support for a serious bid to do that and make United a fan-owned club.

The money stays in your account. The number on this page is what people have said they would commit, not what has been paid. If a purchase ever becomes possible, we will then ask you to pay what you pledged.

If this gains traction and becomes a close to a reality, we will setup a legal structure that would make it impossible for the club to be taken over by a wealthy group catering to their own benefit ever again. More on those proposed structure will be published on this site in the coming days. The proposal will be up for public comments and modified accordingly

Key Facts

  1. No money yetNothing is collected until there is a legal structure and published rules.
  2. No guaranteeA pledge is not a takeover, a share, or a return.
  3. One voteEvery member pledging $1000 or more, gets exactly one vote.

      About

      There is no hiding. Manchester United as an institution has been destroyed by the Glazer ownership. More than a billion pounds taken away from the club in dividends and debt while investing no money of their own. The INEOS ownership has brought some structure, but is ultimately paralyzed by the conditions it has found the club in.

      We, the fans, call ourselves the biggest club in the world- but the results and performances over the last 13 years have been far from that. Managers come and go but the story remains the same. Because the problem is not on the field, but off-it, above it. The only goal of the current ownership is economic profit. This can't go on forever. This will not go on for ever. As fans, we deserve better.

      We can't wait for some wealthy investor to save us. Who knows what their motives will be? We have to do it ourselves. The reason Bayern Munich, Real Madrid, and Barcelona maintain their success on the pitch is because they are fan-owned. Their board's sole purpose is success on the pitch. The only way we ensure lasting success for us would be to implement that structure at United as well.

      This won't be easy. Neither the Glazers nor the INEOS would want to sell easily. It might lead to some difficult seasons on the horizon as well, but we need to think in decades here. Lets face it, we are not winning the premier league anytime soon anyway. We need to make this happen- so that 10 years from now, we are not stuck in the same cycle as we have been.

      FAQ

      Is any money being taken?

      No. A pledge records what you would pay later. Nothing is charged, and the page has no payment button.

      Does a pledge buy a share or a vote?

      No. It is a statement of intent. A share, a vote, and any legal structure would have to be created separately, and you would be asked again before any money moved.

      What is the $10 billion figure?

      It is the working target for buying the club and clearing its debt, not cash in hand. The large number is the sum of pledges. Still to raise is that target minus the sum.

      Who can see my details?

      Your email, phone number, age, and legal name stay off the page. Your display name, country, and amount appear only if you tick the public box.

      What if the owners never sell?

      The register can still show the scale of support. It does not force a sale, and a pledge does not become a debt you owe.
